As models rapidly improve and make more things possible, there's a growing need to make the interactions between humans and AI more personalized. AI should be reimagined as a collaborator that works alongside humans — taking in feedback and a range of inputs to dynamically shape what it produces.

This has clear applications in personalized training, but more broadly, the goal is to redefine the modality of human–AI interaction itself.

At [Palette] (YC S26), we want to dive deeper into this space and explore how the video modality can be dynamically constructed — drawing on computer vision and spatial intelligence research, alongside the engineering needed to build the infrastructure that powers human–AI interaction.

Inspiration for this direction comes in part from [humans&], a startup founded by fellow MIT grads.
